For each book, decide whether one candidate is the same book:
- "match": one candidate is the same book. Give its candidate_id.
- "ambiguous": several candidates fit and nothing on the spine tells them apart, for example different editions.
- "none": no candidate fits.

Prefer "none" to a doubtful match. A reading may be incomplete, or differ in script or spelling from the catalogue.

Do not use tools. Do not look anything up. Do not stop to ask a question.

Answer with one JSON object and nothing else, in this shape:
{"picks": [{"item": 0, "verdict": "match | ambiguous | none", "candidate_id": "<id or null>", "reason": "<one short sentence>"}]}
Give one pick for each book.
